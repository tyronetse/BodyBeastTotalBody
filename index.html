🦍 Body Beast: Total Body

~38 min • 4 circuits • 2 rounds each • 15 reps

Equipment: Dumbbells, bench, mat, pull-up bar

💡 Sagi's tip: go heavier on the second round of each exercise.

Circuit 1

2 rounds × 15 reps

1. Pull-Up15 reps
2. Push-Up15 reps
3. Squat15 reps
4. Crunch15 reps

Circuit 2

2 rounds × 15 reps

1. Incline Dumbbell Press15 reps
2. Bent-Over Row15 reps
3. Reverse Alternating Lunge15 reps

Alternate legs, step back into lunge

4. Plank Twist-Twist15 reps

From plank, bring each knee toward opposite elbow

Circuit 3

2 rounds × 15 reps

1. 1-1-2 Military Press15 reps

Left arm, right arm, then both together = 1 rep

2. Single-Arm Rear Delt Raise15 each arm
3. Stiff-Leg Deadlift15 reps
4. Russian Twist (weighted)15 each side

Circuit 4

2 rounds × 15 reps

1. Bicep Curl Up / Hammer Down15 reps

Curl up supinated, lower down with hammer grip

2. Single-Arm Tricep Kickback15 each arm
3. Calf Raise15 reps

Hold dumbbell at shoulder

4. Side Forearm Plank15 each side

Cool down & stretch. You earned it. 💪
